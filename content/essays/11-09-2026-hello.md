## What this site is for

To make economic information usable by people who are not economists. Public finance in
India is not short of numbers. It is short of numbers anyone can find, check and reuse.

## A table, to check it behaves on a phone

Fiscal tables are wide, and a wide table is the usual reason a page drags sideways on a
phone. On this site a table scrolls inside its own box while the page stays still. Narrow
the window until this one scrolls, and confirm the page itself does not.

| Year | Vintage | Receipts | Expenditure | Deficit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2023-24 | Actuals | 27,88,872 | 44,42,542 | 16,53,670 |
| 2024-25 | Revised Estimates | 30,87,000 | 47,16,000 | 16,29,000 |
| 2025-26 | Budget Estimates | 34,20,409 | 50,65,345 | 16,44,936 |

Figures are illustrative and should not be cited. The `Vintage` column is the point: Budget
Estimates, Revised Estimates and Actuals for the same year are three different facts, not
one figure corrected twice. Conflating them is the most common error in Indian fiscal
datasets, and it quietly corrupts every comparison built on top.
