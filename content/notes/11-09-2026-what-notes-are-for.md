A note is something worth keeping at a permanent address under my own name, but not worth
structuring with sections and citations. A reaction to a figure the day it is released. A
clarification of something I got asked twice. A pointer to a document more people should
read.

The difference from an essay is one of weight, not of care. If a note grows past about a
thousand words, acquires a table, or starts needing footnotes, it becomes an essay and
moves to `content/essays/` — and that move happens before it is ever published, because
once a URL exists it does not get to change.
