# data/local/

Datasets about **municipal and panchayat finances** — the third tier.

**This folder may stay empty for a long time, and that is fine.** It exists so that the
first local-body dataset has an obvious home rather than being filed under `states/` and
setting a bad precedent that is painful to undo later.

## What belongs here

- Urban local body budget and accounts compilations
- Municipal corporation budgets, where a usable series exists
- Panchayat finance data
- Local body grants, as received by the bodies themselves
- State Finance Commission reports and their recommended shares

## What does not

- A state government's **transfers to** local bodies, seen from the state's own accounts —
  that is state finance and goes in `../states/`. The test is whose accounts the numbers
  come from.
- Census or survey data about municipalities — `../surveys/`.

## A warning specific to this group

Local body finance data in India is genuinely bad: incomplete, inconsistently formatted,
published irregularly and often only as scanned PDFs. A dataset here is likely to need more
documentation of what is **missing** than of what is present.

When that happens, say so plainly in the README and in `METHOD.md`. Coverage gaps stated
openly are useful. Coverage gaps left for the reader to discover are how a dataset becomes
misleading.
